# Python Judge asset credits

- `judge.glb`: **Military tactical suit (LowPolyGameReady)** by **DanlyVostok (1799danly)**, [original](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/military-tactical-suit-lowpolygameready-ef698ce36b1545a78ce592dd3db4c7ed), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This copy comes from the [3d-fps asset collection](https://github.com/cktang88/3d-fps/tree/130ac2a5c856401ec36e7d35afcfefe40c5801f4). The upstream copy merges meshes and converts textures to WebP. This interface shades the uniform darker and omits the floating headset.
- `judge-motion.glb`: non-visual rig from **FREE [Military Soldier] RIGGED** by **BAMEN**, [original](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-military-soldier-rigged-e9c56308a67d4a3db62e914fafa4d198), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Includes six selected clips from the [same upstream asset collection](https://github.com/cktang88/3d-fps/tree/130ac2a5c856401ec36e7d35afcfefe40c5801f4), credited upstream to the [Universal Animation Library by Quaternius and Gonzalo Furnier](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Model geometry and other clips were removed from the motion file. Three.js retargets the chosen clips to the tactical suit at runtime. No 100STYLE clips are included.
- `pulse-prop.obj` and `pulse-prop.jpg`: model 1 from [Scifi Gun Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/scifi-gun-pack). Concepts by **Nicolas Mina**, models by **Teh_Bucket**, [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). These are the original geometry and 512 px texture. The original `pulse-prop-license.txt` is included. The prop is animated and lit as fictional game scenery.
- Sounds are synthesized with the Web Audio API. Glass, particles, interface, and cinematic timing are implemented in this frontend.
